DISCIPLINE 03 // HIGH-TORQUE TUG PROTOCOL

ROBO TUG OF WAR.

Maximum torque transmission and high-friction tire mechanics in a direct tether pulling duel across a steel deck.

MISSION BRIEFING

Two robots are linked via a 1.5-meter high-tensile steel cable on an elevated steel battle deck. Upon arena start, machines must apply continuous tractive force to pull the opponent across the center line.

Success requires planetary gearboxes with high reduction ratios, custom silicone or polyurethane tread compounds, and precise weight distribution.
