01 // ELIGIBILITY & TEAM STRUCTURE

Pilot Roster & Academic Status

  • 1.1
    Institution Enrollment: Open exclusively to bona fide students of Army Public School, Lucknow across Classes 6 through 12.
  • 1.2
    Team Composition: Teams may consist of a minimum of 1 member (solo builder/pilot) up to a maximum of 4 registered students. Cross-section and inter-class teams are fully permitted.
  • 1.3
    Machine Exclusivity: A single physical chassis cannot be shared across multiple registered team names.
02 // ROBOT SPECIFICATIONS

Chassis Envelope, Mass & Power

  • 2.1
    Dimensional Envelope: The robot must fit entirely inside a 300 mm (Length) × 300 mm (Width) × 250 mm (Height) inspection box in ready-to-run state.
  • 2.2
    Mass Constraint: Maximum weight limit is 3.00 kg (including all batteries, transmitters, and armor).
  • 2.3
    Electrical Limits: Maximum open-circuit DC bus voltage must not exceed 12.6V (3S LiPo or 12V Lead-Acid/NiMH equivalent).
  • 2.4
    Control Systems: Permitted wireless protocols include 2.4 GHz RF, Bluetooth, ESP-NOW, Wi-Fi, and tethered wired remotes (with minimum 3m non-interfering cord).
03 // RACE PROCEDURE

Track Protocol & Trial Runs

  • 3.1
    Staging & Calibration: Each team will be provided a 60-second staging window on the start line before the official green signal.
  • 3.2
    Two Official Runs: Each machine gets two independent timed runs over the course. The single fastest valid run is recorded for final standings.
  • 3.3
    Obstacle Traversal: Robots must negotiate all official track features: speed chicanes, 15° inclined ramp, bridge pass, and loose-gravel terrain.
04 // SAFETY DIRECTIVES

Battery Security & Operational Safety

  • 4.1
    Firm Battery Enclosure: Lithium batteries must be rigidly strapped down with velcro or locked compartments to prevent ejection during high-speed rollovers.
  • 4.2
    Emergency Cutoff: A master power switch or accessible quick-pull main battery disconnect connector must be readily visible on the top deck.
05 // PENALTIES & FOULS

Track Deviations & Manual Resets

  • 5.1
    Manual Touch / Reset (+5.0s): If a robot flips, stalls, or leaves the track boundary, a designated student handler may reset it to the previous checkpoint with a +5.00s penalty.
  • 5.2
    Max Resets: A maximum of 3 manual resets are allowed per trial run. A fourth failure results in DNF (Did Not Finish).
  • 5.3
    Track Surface Damage: Any device intended to cut, gouge, or burn the track surface will cause immediate disqualification.
06 // SCORING MATRIX & PODIUM CRITERIA

Final Time Calculation

  • 6.1
    Final Adjusted Score: Score = Raw Lap Time + (Manual Resets × 5.00s) + Obstacle Skip Penalties. Lowest total time wins 1st Place.
  • 6.2
    Tiebreaker: In the event of a tie to hundredths of a second, the second fastest clean trial run will determine the winner.
  • 6.3
    Referee Final Authority: The decisions of the Chief Race Marshal and Tinkering & Robotics Lab mentors are final and binding.
