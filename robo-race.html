DISCIPLINE 01 // SPEED & TIME ATTACK

ROBO RACE.

A high-octane battle against the stopwatch. Machines navigate banked curves, elevated gravel ramps, speed bridges, and slalom chicanes in minimum time.

MISSION BRIEFING

Robo Race tests the limits of robot maneuverability, torque-to-weight ratio, wireless latency, and pilot reflexes. Robots compete individually in timed trial runs across a multi-segment custom engineered track.

Points are awarded based on completion time, clean obstacle clearance, and flawless checkpoint crossings without manual resets.

TRACK TELEMETRY CIRCUIT LENGTH: 48 METERS
OBSTACLES: Slalom Cones • Gravel Pit • 15° Incline Ramp • Speed Switchback
