01 // ELIGIBILITY & TEAM ROSTER

Combat Crew Structure

  • 1.1
    Student Pilots: Registered APS Lucknow students (Classes 6–12). Teams can have 1 to 4 members including Driver, Weapon Operator, and Pit Technician.
  • 1.2
    Safety Verification: Every combat bot must clear mandatory pre-fight bench inspection and radio failsafe testing by the Lab safety marshal.
02 // ROBOT SPECIFICATIONS & MASS

Armor Enclosure & 5.00 KG Limit

  • 2.1
    Maximum Weight: 5.00 kg strict weight limit (tolerance +50g for calibration difference).
  • 2.2
    Dimensional Footprint: Must pass through the arena inspection gate (500 mm × 500 mm × 400 mm).
  • 2.3
    Power Storage: Maximum 14.8V nominal (4S LiPo/LiFePO4 or equivalent sealed battery packs). Batteries must be encased within internal armor plates.
03 // MATCH PROCEDURE & ARENA PROTOCOL

3-Minute Knockout Combat

  • 3.1
    Combat Duration: 3 minutes of continuous active combat inside the sealed polycarbonate safety arena.
  • 3.2
    Knockout Condition (KO): If a machine ceases translational motion (inability to move out of a circle equal to its radius) for 10 seconds, the referee begins the 10-count countdown to declare KO.
  • 3.3
    Arena Hazards: Floor flipper pits and hazard spikes are active throughout combat.
04 // SAFETY DIRECTIVES & FAILSAFES

Mandatory Weapon Safety Locks

  • 4.1
    Mechanical Safety Pin: Spinning weapons must have a visible locking bar/pin with a bright red streamer attached during pit prep.
  • 4.2
    Radio Failsafe (R/C): In the event of radio link loss or transmitter power off, weapon motors and drive motors must automatically drop to 0% throttle within 1.0 second.
05 // PROHIBITED WEAPONS & HAZARDS

Illegal Weapon Classes

  • 5.1
    Strictly Prohibited: Open fire/flame, explosives, untethered projectiles, liquid spray/chemicals, EMP/radio jamming devices, and high-voltage taser contacts.
  • 5.2
    Permitted Mechanisms: Spinning drums, horizontal blades, pneumatic flippers, motorized lifting arms, circular carbide saws, and static wedge plow armor.
06 // SCORING MATRIX & JUDGES DIRECTIVES

Judges’ 10-Point Score Matrix

  • 6.1
    Damage (5 Points): Structural, kinematic, and functional damage inflicted upon the opponent’s robot via weapon contact.
  • 6.2
    Aggression (3 Points): Frequency of attacking engagements with active weapons versus evasive backing.
  • 6.3
    Control (2 Points): Dominance of arena positioning, avoiding hazard zones, and driving precision.
