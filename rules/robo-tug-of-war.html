01 // ELIGIBILITY & TEAM ROSTER

Academic & Pilot Standards

  • 1.1
    School Roster: Open to all active students of Army Public School Lucknow (Classes 6 to 12).
  • 1.2
    Team Allocation: 1 to 4 members per pulling team.
02 // ROBOT SPECIFICATIONS & TETHER POINT

Mass Limit & Hitch Geometry

  • 2.1
    Weight Category: Maximum gross mass of 5.00 kg.
  • 2.2
    Rear Hitch Eyelet: Machines must feature a rigid rear eyelet (internal diameter ≥ 12mm) located at a height of 50 mm to 100 mm above the arena surface for secure carabiner tether attachment.
  • 2.3
    Power Supply: Maximum voltage limit of 14.8V DC.
03 // MATCH PROCEDURE & STEEL DECK PROTOCOL

90-Second Continuous Pulling Bout

  • 3.1
    Arena Setup: The duel takes place on an elevated 4.0m × 1.2m steel battle platform. A 1.5-meter high-tensile steel cable connects the machines across the central zero-marker ribbon.
  • 3.2
    Tension Pre-Check: Both teams reverse slowly until the steel tether is taut. The match referee signals the countdown to start motor torque.
04 // TRACTION & TREAD DIRECTIVES

Permitted Compounds & Chemical Bans

  • 4.1
    Compliant Treads: Solid rubber, high-grip silicone tires, and polyurethane continuous tracks are fully compliant.
  • 4.2
    Prohibited Substances: Liquid adhesives, sticky resin coatings, vacuum suction fans, magnets, or steel claws that gouge the deck are strictly banned.
05 // PENALTIES & FOULS

Premature Pulls & Cable Disconnects

  • 5.1
    False Start: Applying full motor throttle before the referee’s whistle results in one warning. A second false start concedes the round.
  • 5.2
    Hitch Failure: If a robot’s towing hitch breaks under tension, the opponent is awarded the round win.
06 // SCORING & PODIUM VICTORY

Pull Over Central Baseline

  • 6.1
    Clean Victory: Pulling the entirety of the opponent robot across the central threshold within 90 seconds wins the bout immediately.
  • 6.2
    Time Expiry: If neither bot completely crosses within 90 seconds, whichever bot pulled the central ribbon further into its half wins by distance displacement.
